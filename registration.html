Section 03

Important Registration Notes

Please read these before you register.

  1. Registration is considered complete only after successful payment and submission of the online registration form.
  2. Participants must complete the payment before filling out the registration form.
  3. Please retain the payment receipt and transaction reference number for future communication.
  4. Authors presenting accepted papers must follow the conference's paper registration and presentation requirements.
  5. Please use a valid email address and mobile number for all conference communications.
