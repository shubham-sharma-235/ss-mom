01 What We're Looking For

Submitted manuscripts should present original research, innovative methodologies, theoretical developments, experimental results, modelling and simulation, empirical findings, industrial applications, or significant case studies relevant to one or more conference tracks.

Submitted manuscripts must:

  • Be original and unpublished
  • Not be under consideration for publication elsewhere
  • Fall within the scope of SS-MOM 2027
  • Clearly identify the corresponding author
  • Include the affiliation and email address of all authors
  • Follow the prescribed manuscript template and submission guidelines
  • Meet the required standards of academic quality and research ethics

03 Peer-Review Process

All submitted manuscripts will undergo a rigorous peer-review process, which broadly consists of:

  1. Initial Screening
  2. Technical Peer Review
  3. Revision
  4. Final Evaluation
  5. Acceptance

Each submission will first undergo an editorial screening to assess its scope, originality, quality, and compliance with submission requirements.

Manuscripts meeting the initial requirements will be assigned to appropriate reviewers with relevant subject expertise. Authors may be requested to revise their manuscripts based on reviewer comments.

The final decision regarding acceptance will be made by the conference's Technical Program Committee/Editorial Committee based on the reviewers' recommendations and the overall quality and relevance of the manuscript.

06 Author Guidelines

Authors are requested to carefully follow the conference manuscript template and formatting instructions.

≤ 18 pagesSingle column, 1.5 line spacing
≤ 250 wordsAbstract length
A4 · 2.5 cmEuropean A4, all margins
400 / 600 dpiImage figures / line art

Content & scope

  • Manuscripts must present original research and should not have been previously published.
  • The manuscript should clearly state the title, abstract, keywords, authors, affiliations, and corresponding author's email address.
  • The manuscript should fall within the scope of one of the three conference tracks.
  • Contents of papers presented should lie within the general scope of the conference.
  • The abstract should not exceed 250 words.

Length & layout

  • Full-length original research contributions and review articles should not exceed 18 pages in single column format, with 1.5 line spacing.
  • Paper size should be European A4.
  • Margins should be 2.5 cm (top and bottom) and 2.5 cm (left and right).
  • There should be no page numbers, or headers and footers, within the paper.
  • All pages should be portrait.

Figures

  • Figures in image format should be of minimum 400 dpi.
  • Line art figures in image format should be of minimum 600 dpi.
  • Figures should be placed within the text and should not be collected at the end of the document.

File format

  • The paper should be submitted in .pdf format without any password protection.
  • The PDF should be free from formatting errors (e.g. corrupt equations, missing or low-resolution figures), since conversion from Word to PDF can introduce formatting errors.

Language & permissions

  • A thorough proofread should be conducted to check the standard of English and ensure that wording is clear and concise.
  • Ensure that all permissions have been obtained for any material within the manuscript that is not from the submitted original work, or from previously published work.

Review & survey papers

  • Literature review / survey papers should present a totally new vision in the respective field, or at least benefit the field with a new perspective, if they are to be published.

References & citations

  • The reference list should be checked for accuracy. References can only be linked via Crossref if they are correct and complete.
  • Authors are advised to follow the APA style of reference citation.
  • All the references given in the list of references should be cited in the body of the text.
  • Citations should be given by number, whether sequential by order of citation or according to the sequence in an alphabetized list. For example:
Single citation [9] Multiple citation [4–6, 9]

Multiple citations should be in numerical order. With sequential citation by order of citation, reference 7 cannot be cited before reference 5.

07 Originality and Similarity

10% Maximum similarity index. Every paper is checked with Turnitin.

All submitted papers will go through a "Plagiarism & Similarity test" by Turnitin software. Papers with more than 10% similarity index will not be examined and will face rejection / withdrawal without a formal review.

Manuscripts containing substantial similarity, plagiarism, duplicate publication, fabricated or manipulated data, or other forms of academic misconduct may be rejected in accordance with applicable publication ethics and conference policies.
