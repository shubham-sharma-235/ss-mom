Sponsorship Opportunities

Partner with SS-MOM 2027

Showcase Your Innovation. Connect with Experts. Shape the Future of Sustainable Industry.

The 1st International Conference on Smart and Sustainable Manufacturing, Operations, and Materials (SS-MOM 2027) invites industries, technology companies, research organisations, startups, professional bodies, and corporate partners to join us as Sponsors and Industry Partners.

Scheduled for 04–05 February 2027 at Manipal University Jaipur, SS-MOM 2027 will bring together researchers, academicians, industry professionals, engineers, technology experts, PhD scholars, and students to explore emerging developments in smart manufacturing, sustainable operations, industrial engineering, advanced materials, Industry 4.0/5.0, AI, digital transformation, and circular economy.

04–05 February 2027 Manipal University Jaipur

Sponsoring SS-MOM 2027 lets your organisation:

  • Increase brand visibility among an international academic and professional audience
  • Showcase products, technologies, solutions, and innovations
  • Engage directly with researchers, engineers, faculty members, and industry leaders
  • Explore opportunities for industry–academia collaboration
  • Connect with emerging talent and prospective employees
  • Position your organisation as a supporter of sustainable and smart industrial transformation
  • Promote your organisation through conference communication and promotional platforms
