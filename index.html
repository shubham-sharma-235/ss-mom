About SS-Mom 2027 2027

SS-MOM 2027 is an international platform for researchers, PhD scholars, postdoctoral fellows, professors, scientists, and industry experts to share cutting-edge research in smart, digital, and sustainable industrial systems. It focuses on emerging technologies including AI/ML, digital twins, cyber-physical systems, IoT, Industry 4.0/5.0, and sustainable manufacturing, applied to materials, manufacturing, and operational systems.

The conference promotes interdisciplinary collaboration and supports the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) by exploring energy-efficient, environmentally responsible, and resource-optimized engineering solutions.

Participants will benefit from:

● Opportunities to publish in Scopus-indexed proceedings and high-impact journals
● Networking with international researchers, industry leaders, and journal editors
● Participation in workshops and tutorials on AI/ML, Digital Twins, and sustainable manufacturing
● Insights into industry applications, including smart factories, predictive maintenance, additive and hybrid manufacturing, and circular economy initiatives

About Manipal University Jaipur

The Manipal Education and Medical Group is an established leader in the fields of education, research, and healthcare. In a span of over seven decades, it has transformed the lives of more than 300,000 students from over 59 countries.

The group includes five universities: Manipal Academy of Higher Education (MAHE, Karnataka), Sikkim Manipal University (Sikkim), American University of Antigua (Caribbean Islands), Manipal International University (Malaysia), and Manipal University Jaipur (MUJ).

Manipal University Jaipur (MUJ) was launched in 2011 on an invitation from the Government of Rajasthan as a self-financed state private university. MUJ has redefined academic excellence in the region with the Manipal way of learning, inspiring students across disciplines to learn and innovate through hands-on practical experience.

Jaipur, being one of the fastest-growing cities in India, has an increasing demand for quality higher education in the region. Following the allotment of 122 acres of land at Dehmi Kalan village near Jaipur, the permanent campus has developed rapidly and is regarded as one of the best campuses in the region.

The multidisciplinary university offers career-oriented courses at all levels, including UG, PG, and doctoral programs, across diverse streams such as engineering, architecture, planning, fashion design, interior design, fine arts, hospitality, humanities, journalism and mass communication, basic sciences, law, commerce, computer applications, and management. Some PG programs are also available in the research mode.

MUJ boasts best-in-class infrastructure, including state-of-the-art research facilities and a modern digital library. In line with the legacy of Manipal University, the campus uses the latest technology to provide quality education and a future-ready learning environment.

Department of Mechanical Engineering

Mechanical Engineering is a diverse and dynamic field, encompassing everything from the design of intricate components to the development of large-scale systems and machines. Mechanical engineers play a vital role across a wide range of industries, including aerospace, automotive, electronics, energy, construction, consumer products, and government sectors. Emerging fields like medical and pharmaceutical engineering also offer exciting career opportunities.

The B.Tech. in Mechanical Engineering program equips students with the skills and knowledge to design and build the machines of the future. With access to state-of-the-art laboratories and a well-stocked library, students gain both strong theoretical foundations and valuable hands-on practical experience. The program also supports lateral entry, offering flexibility for diploma holders to join and advance their education.

Mechanical engineers also make significant contributions to the defense sector, where they are involved in the design and development of advanced weapon systems, armored vehicles, propulsion systems, drones, and surveillance equipment.

In addition to undergraduate studies, the department also offers a rigorous Ph.D. program that fosters advanced research in both core and interdisciplinary areas of mechanical engineering. The program provides scholars with opportunities to contribute to cutting-edge innovations through high-impact research, guided by experienced faculty and supported by well-equipped research laboratories.

How to Reach MUJ

By Road

Jaipur is well linked by a good network of road transport to all important cities. There are excellent bus services between Jaipur – Delhi running approximately every half an hour both ways.

  • Delhi to Jaipur: ~5–6 hours
  • Bus Stand to MUJ: 25 Kms

By Train

The Jaipur Railway Station is centrally located and well connected to all major cities across India. Overnight trains regularly arrive from major hubs like Delhi, Agra, Chennai, and Mumbai.

  • Cost-effective & overnight options
  • Railway Station to MUJ: 24 Kms
Prepaid taxis and cabs are readily available at the station.

By Air

Jaipur Airport is located in Sanganer and features robust domestic air links connecting directly with major Indian cities including Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Hyderabad, and Kolkata.

  • Well-connected domestic terminal
  • Jaipur Airport to MUJ: 27 Kms

The Pink City

Explore Jaipur

Jaipur — famously known as the "Paris of India" and the "Pink City" — is a major tourist destination in India, forming part of the Golden Triangle along with Delhi and Agra. The city is renowned for its palaces, royalty, forts, culture, and delectable Rajasthani cuisine. Its aesthetically precise architecture is reflected in UNESCO World Heritage Sites such as the City Palace, Albert Hall Museum, Hawa Mahal, Jal Mahal, Jaigarh Fort, Nahargarh Fort, and Amber Fort. This pink-walled city also showcases exceptional artisanship through flamboyant bazaars famous for exotic wares, tie-dye scarves, blue pottery, jewelry, and leather-embroidered shoes. During February, the weather is pleasant with clear skies and chilly nights — perfect for sightseeing, with a refreshing cool breeze and no monsoon rain.
