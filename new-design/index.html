About Manipal University Jaipur

Manipal University Jaipur is one of India's leading multidisciplinary universities with world-class infrastructure, research facilities, innovation centers, and academic excellence inspired by the globally recognized Manipal legacy.

The Department of Mechanical Engineering actively promotes cutting-edge research in manufacturing, smart systems, sustainability, advanced materials, robotics, automation, and industrial engineering.
