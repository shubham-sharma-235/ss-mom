Publication Opportunities

Share your research. Extend its impact.

SS-MOM 2027 brings together research in smart and sustainable manufacturing, operations, and materials. The conference offers eligible authors a pathway to disseminate their work through scholarly proceedings, along with opportunities to develop selected research into full journal articles.

01 Conference Proceedings 02 Extended Journal Articles 03 Support for Journal Publications
Section 01

Conference proceedings

Accepted, registered, and presented papers will be submitted for publication in book proceedings of:

Elsevier · Wiley · Taylor & Francis / Routledge · De Gruyter Brill · Springer Nature

Each paper will have its own DOI, as with Scopus conference proceedings, and will be indexed in the Scopus database.

The appropriate proceedings outlet will be determined by the paper’s subject, quality, and the requirements of the relevant publisher or series.

All papers shall be published in proceedings as mentioned above without any extra charges. Your registration includes the presentation of one accepted paper at the SS-MOM-2027 conference, publication of one accepted paper in Scopus-indexed conference proceedings, access to all sessions (keynote/special track, and parallel sessions) at the conference, conference kits, and lunch during conference days.

Section 03

Support for journal publications

The editorial committee and organizers will decide which manuscripts to submit to journals based on the quality of the work and available publication options. The organizers aim to publish more and more papers as journal articles, subject to further revision of the extended manuscript.

Each special issue or journal collection will have its own theme, scope, submission instructions, and deadline. Only the Organizer will decide and invite authors for the possible options based on certain factors and feasibility. Authors are free to decide whether to prepare and submit an extended article.

Authors are responsible for applicable charges unless a partial or full waiver or support is confirmed by the organizers. However, the updated list of journals associated with the conference will be updated from time to time, and APC support may be provided by the organizers for some journals and selected high-quality papers, as per the publisher's agreement with the organizers.
